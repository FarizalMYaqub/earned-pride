After a year of stuttering through every assembly, eight-year-old Jun read the morning announcements to the whole school without once looking down at his shoes. This program writes that one quiet line to `/output/entry.txt` for the judge — the line itself is the whole entry, nothing more. It stays inside the gate: one sentence, 160 characters, with the pride arriving in the last detail instead of being announced. The setup is a year of difficulty the reader can feel; the payoff is a small boy who finally keeps his eyes up.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
